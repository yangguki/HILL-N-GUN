## 1) Hero Raven (chibi dress)

The adult Quaternius cowboy mesh is still used for bones/Animator, but its SkinnedMeshRenderers are hidden after `FitHeight`. A procedural chubby chibi is built in **local space of the 1-unit body** (wide head, short body, cel ramp + outline, higher-segment smoothed primitives in `Proto_Greybox3DSmoothPrim`).

Bone-follow was abandoned for the silhouette: in edit-mode simulate the pack bones stay near the origin, so the dress stacked into a blob at the feet. `DressFollow` remains for any future attachments; the visible outfit no longer depends on it.

From behind the hero now reads as:

- Wide round-brim purple hat with a red band and a short dented crown
- Dark brown hair filling under the brim (back and sides)
- Red bandana knot + hanging tails
- Purple shirt with sleeves
- Brown belt, buckle, holster and leather straps
- Blue jeans and brown boots
- Silver revolver in the right hand

## 2) Grunts

Same chibi construction, 3 subtle variants (hat brim cut + vest/hat tint). Brown cowboy hat, red bandana over the lower face/neck, tan/brown vest, blue jeans, brown boots, pistol raised in the right hand.

## 3) Bullets

Sizes unchanged from E/F (ally visual ≈ 0.25 hero width, enemy ≈ 0.33).

- **Enemy:** glossy red capsule along travel (+Z), orange-yellow flame puffs and a short comet trail, soft glow. Glow intensity was pulled back so the body stays red instead of blowing out to a white spark.
- **Ally:** small gold capsule, brass tip, short yellow streak, cel outline.

## 4) Houses

Store count is **9**, not the packed 11: **3 per side** staggered along the street plus **3 at the far end**, with desert/cacti gaps (minimum spacing asserted in LookTests). Each is a western false-front storefront: plank-strip walls, stepped or peaked parapet, framed windows with panes, door, porch posts, 5-stripe awning (blue / orange / red / yellow), sign board. Heights and warm brown tints vary. No boxy stacked blocks.

## 5) Mesas

The old floating `MesaBand` stripes are gone. Nine sculpted buttes (`Greybox3D_Mesa`) sit at two depths: tapering cylindrical strata, a flat cap, darker bases, and a haze lerp toward sky blue on the far row.

## 6) Sky and grade

The pale Kenney panoramic is **no longer** assigned as `RenderSettings.skybox` (it was washing the upper frame). The camera uses the procedural Sky shader as the skybox: deep saturated blue at the zenith, lighter cyan-blue near the horizon, `_UseTex` 0. The sky dome is flipped / Cull Off so it still draws if the shader falls back. Bigger white cartoon clouds sit inside the gameplay frustum (they used to be above the top of the frame) and use unlit white/soft-blue so they stay bright. Key/ambient and vignette warmth stay in the vivid, slightly warm grade from F/G.
