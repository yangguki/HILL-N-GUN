## What changed

The placeholder primitive view is replaced with CC0 meshes, cel shading, and clips driven by existing combat state.

- **Hero / grunt:** Quaternius Ultimate Animated Character Pack (2019) `Cowboy_Male.fbx`, instanced from a Resources prefab. Hero recolor: purple hat + shirt, red bandana, blue jeans. Grunt: one shared outfit (brown hat, tan shirt, red bandana, blue jeans). 12+, no blood.
- **Animation:** Playables mixer (`Proto_Greybox3DAnimDriver`) — idle, walk (sped for move), shoot, hit. Roll stays a procedural spin; down holds the hit pose. Clips on the FBX: `CharacterArmature|Idle`, `Walk`, `Shoot_OneHanded`, `RecieveHit`, `Punch`. No dedicated run/death/roll clip in the pack.
- **Background:** Procedural western storefronts with wood texture and colored awnings (main row + side wings), continuous rail fence with a center gap (Kenney fence segments packed as extra detail), crates/barrels, cacti, rocks, grass tufts, mesa blocks, solid warm sand (the 128px dirt tex was not tiled on the playfield — it turned into a giant checker), Kenney day skybox + gradient sky sphere.
- **Look:** Built-in RP cel ramp with stronger saturation, **world-space** inverted-hull outline (object-space width exploded on the FBX’s ×100 mesh), soft blob shadows, warm key + blue fill. Ally bullets: small yellow glow. Enemy bullets: larger red/orange glow. 3D grenade arc (8 dots) + landing ring. Thin Greybox hook: `GrenadeAimPreviewActive` / `GrenadePreviewLanding`.
- **Camera:** Lower and closer than slice A — just above hat height, nearly level, hero at the bottom-center, fence and enemy row visible, sky band at the top. Grunt min height raised so they read past the fence.
- Catalog: `Resources/HillNGunGreybox3D/Catalog` filled by `HillNGun/Proto/Greybox3D/Rebuild Catalog`. No new heavy packages; FBX imported natively (no glTF / Blender).
